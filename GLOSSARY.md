# Tabaqat

The people and lineage relationships represented in Tabaqat.

## Language

**Person**:
A historical figure with a stable identity, a display name, and optional aliases and honorifics.

**Lineage tree**:
A representation of descent through father-to-child connections. Each person has at most one father in the tree; spouses and mothers are described in profiles.
_Avoid_: Relationship network

**Father-to-child connection**:
A relationship in which the source person is the father of the destination person.

**Alias**:
An alternative name associated with the same person, rather than a separate person.

**Person role**:
A designation of a person's religious status or historical office, such as prophet or Rashidun caliph. A person may hold more than one role.

**Rashidun caliph**:
A person who served as caliph during the Rashidun period. In Tabaqat, this includes Abū Bakr, ʿOmar, ʿUthmān, ʿAlī, and Al-Ḥasan.

**Period**:
A named historical grouping, such as Rashidun, Umayyad, or Abbasid, whose caliphs and related family branches are considered together.

**Period lineage tree**:
A lineage tree showing the family branches associated with a selected period, together with ancestors needed to explain their connections. It represents descent, not the order in which caliphs ruled.

**Reign**:
A span of years during which a person held the caliphate or claimed it.

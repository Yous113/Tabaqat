# Tabaqat

The people and lineage relationships represented in Tabaqat.

## Language

**Person**:
A historical figure with a stable identity, a display name, and optional aliases and honorifics.

**Lineage tree**:
A representation of descent through father-to-child connections. Each person has at most one father in the tree; spouses and mothers are described in profiles.
_Avoid_: Relationship network

**Father-to-child connection**:
A relationship in which the source person is the father of the destination person.

**Alias**:
An alternative name associated with the same person, rather than a separate person.

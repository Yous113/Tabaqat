# The graph is a strict father-to-child tree

Tabaqat shows lineage as a tree: every person has at most one parent, their father. Teacher, spouse and other relationships were considered as extra edges or overlays and rejected, because they turn the layout into a tangled graph and pull the data model toward something much larger than this project needs.

## Consequences

- Mothers and spouses belong in a person's profile, not as connections. A wife such as Khadījah appears in her own father's branch, not beside her husband.
- People with no recorded descent from the root (for example many of the Anṣār, and non-Arab sahaba) can't join this tree. Whether to leave them out or add further roots is still open.
